LOGISTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Logistics Services Agreement is entered into between Globex Logistics
Limited ("Supplier") and Initech Retail Co ("Customer") on 14 June 2024.

1. SCOPE
The Supplier shall provide warehousing, pick-and-pack and last-mile delivery
services across the Customer's northern distribution network.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices Net 60 days from the invoice date.

3. RATES
The agreed rate for senior logistics planners is USD 210 per hour.

4. TERM
The initial term is twenty-four months. Renewal requires written agreement
signed by both parties; this agreement does not renew automatically.
