MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme
Industrial Supply Ltd ("Supplier"), a company registered in England, and
Northwind Trading Co ("Customer") on 1 March 2024.

1. SCOPE
The Supplier shall provide industrial maintenance and professional services
as described in each Statement of Work executed under this Agreement.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices Net 30 days from the invoice date.
Late payment attracts interest at 2% above base rate.

3. RATES
The agreed rate for principal consultants is USD 145 per hour, held firm for
the initial term.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term is twelve months from the effective date. This agreement
renews automatically for successive twelve month periods unless either party
gives written notice ninety days before expiry.

5. LIABILITY
The Supplier's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve
months preceding the claim.
